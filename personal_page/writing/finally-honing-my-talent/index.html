Finally honing my talent

October was the month I stopped thinking about the camera and started carrying it. A short memo of what happened.

25 OCTOBER 2026 · 1 MIN READ

Switched from Premiere Pro to DaVinci Resolve. Not going back.

Got on more projects. People started commissioning me, which still feels new.

Carried my new camera everywhere, got over the FOMO and the imposter syndrome, and actively looked for shots that felt like real life.

Went on rides and worked on projects with friends. Built momentum instead of planning it.
